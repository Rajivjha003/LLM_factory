## 2. Add fusion module

Create:

```text
src/llm_ops/rag/fusion.py
```

Implement:

```text
reciprocal_rank_fusion()
dedupe_by_chunk_id()
normalize_scores()
```

RRF formula:

```text
score = Σ 1 / (k + rank)
```

Default:

```text
RRF_K=60
```

Reason: RRF is stable, simple, and strong enough for Phase 8C.
